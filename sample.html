Step by step

How to submit your game

It's the same whichever AI you used, and takes about three minutes.

  1. Save your game as one file

    Claude: use the download button on the game panel.
    ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot or others: copy all of the code (most code boxes have a copy button), paste it into a plain text editor such as Notepad or TextEdit, and save it with .html at the end, for example sofa-racer.html.

    Double-click the file to check it opens and plays in your browser.

  2. Sign in on the Submit page

    Open Submit and sign in with your work Google account.

  3. Fill in the details

    Your name, the game's title, the AI you used, a one-line description and an emoji. Add your RAY profile picture (required) and, if you like, a screenshot for your gallery card.

  4. Choose your file and check the preview

    The preview runs your game exactly as it will in the gallery. If something's wrong, it tells you what happened and gives you a message to paste into your AI to fix it.

  5. Submit, then share it

    Your game is in the gallery about two minutes later. Share the link in the RAY Connect chat: People's choice needs at least 5 ratings.

  6. Need a fix? You have 2 updates

    Come back to the Submit page: your game is already filled in. Choose the new file, tick "Yes, use an update", and submit. Ratings and scores carry over to the new version.
