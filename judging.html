Judging

How winners are picked

The judges pick the Grand champion and Runner-up. Everyone's star ratings pick People's choice.

Grand champion & Runner-up

The judges' rubric

    CriterionWeightMultiplierWhat judges look for

    Each criterion is scored 1 (didn't land) to 5 (still talking about it), for a total out of 100. A game's score is the judges' average. Ties go to the higher Silliness score. Judges don't score their own game.

    People's choice

    Rate every game you play

    • Sign in, open a game, and give it 1 to 5 stars. You can change your rating until the winners are announced.
    • One rating per game, and not your own.
    • A game needs at least 5 ratings to win. Highest average wins; ties go to the game with more ratings.

    Your ratings are anonymous. Other players and game creators can never see who rated what, so say what you really think.
