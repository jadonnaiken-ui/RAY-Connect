How star ratings work
People's choice

Rate every game you play

Ratings are open now, and the averages are live in the gallery.

Go rate some games
  1. Sign in

    Use your work Google account (top right of any page).

  2. Play, then rate

    Open a game from the gallery and tap 1 to 5 stars at the top. Change your mind any time until the winners are announced.

  3. One rating per game

    You get one rating per game, and you can't rate your own.

  4. Enough votes to qualify

    A game needs at least 5 ratings to win, so one lucky 5-star can't decide it. If two games tie, the one with more ratings wins.

For the judges

The scoring rubric

Each judge scores each game from 1 to 5 per criterion. Each score is multiplied, so a perfect game scores 100.

CriterionWeightMultiplierWhat judges look for
1: Didn't land. Broken, confusing, or the joke fell flat.
3: Solid, got a smile. Works well and raised a grin.
5: Still talking about it. Quoted at lunch the next day.

The final score is the average of the judges' totals. Ties go to the higher Silliness average; if it's still tied, the judges discuss. Judges don't score their own game. Games by "The organisers" are examples and aren't judged.
